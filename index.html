traduct.org

Live, local OCR and translation. Chrome on desktop only.

Chrome asks what to share — pick the window that has the text in it.

This browser has no built-in Translator API, so there is nothing to translate with. Open traduct.org in Chrome 138 or later on the desktop.

Keep the original open. Read the translation right beside it.

How it works

Reading

A PaddleOCR model finds the text in the shared video, running in a worker through eSearch OCR and ONNX Runtime on WebAssembly. It runs entirely on your machine: the models load once from this site, and no pixel is ever sent anywhere.

Translating

Chrome's built-in Translator API translates the recognized text. Chrome documents it as client-side, using models downloaded for your chosen languages and stored on your device. Once those models are ready, you can keep translating without an internet connection.

Drawing

traduct.org measures how far the page scrolled and moves the text it already read, instead of recognizing it again. That is what makes scrolling readable rather than a flicker of re-translations.
